About CINAEDVS

About

A Fusion of Ancient Spirit and Modern Creativity

CINAEDVS is a groundbreaking multimedia brand dedicated to music, video, and digital art production, with a distinctive line of bespoke clothing.

Focusing primarily on dance and pop tracks, CINAEDVS also excels in creating engaging video and digital art for social media and advertising. With a target audience rooted in the queer community of Europe and beyond, CINAEDVS aims to deliver content that resonates deeply with its vibrant and diverse followers.

Drawing inspiration from ancient history, CINAEDVS (pronounced KIN-AYE-DUS) merges historical aesthetics with contemporary flair.

The brand's name, a Latin word derived from “cinaedus,” evokes the image of a gender-deviant entertainer from antiquity, known for his effeminate style and hedonistic allure. This term, which historically referenced an effeminate dancer, reflects the brand's mission to challenge conventional norms and celebrate individuality.

If it be blending ancient chord progressions and historical motifs with modern production techniques and fabrics, CINAEDVS uniquely captures the essence of ancient European artistry and reinvents it for today’s audience.

This innovative approach not only sets CINAEDVS apart but also honors the timeless spirit of cultural and artistic expression, bringing a rich historical legacy into the present and making it accessible to a new generation.
